Defect Handling

The three defect types tracked on the line are scratches, cracks, and pits. A scratch is a thin surface line, a crack is a jagged fracture, and a pit is a small localised hole.

When the defect rate for a shift rises into the top band, quarantine the affected output and inspect the machine that produced it. High defect rates often accompany high vibration or high temperature.

The night shift consistently records a higher defect rate than the day shift, even though output is slightly lower. Supervisors should pay extra attention to night-shift handovers and setup.
