Safety and Lockout

Before any maintenance work, apply lockout/tagout: isolate the machine from power, lock the isolation point, and attach a tag with your name and the date. Never work on a machine that has not been locked out.

Personal protective equipment is mandatory in the maintenance area. The required PPE is safety glasses, cut-resistant gloves, and steel-toe boots.

Only trained and authorised staff may remove a lockout. The person who applied the lock is the person who removes it.
